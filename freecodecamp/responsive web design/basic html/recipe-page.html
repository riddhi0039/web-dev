Classic Veggie Sandwich

This classic veggie sandwich is quick to make, healthy, and perfect for a light meal or snack.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • Fresh tomato slices
  • Cucumber slices
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Butter or mayonnaise

Instructions

  1. Spread butter or mayonnaise evenly on both bread slices.
  2. Place lettuce leaves on one slice of bread.
  3. Add tomato and cucumber slices on top.
  4. Cover with the second slice of bread and serve.
